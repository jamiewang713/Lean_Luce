## Full scope

The objective includes the power-law joint CLT and mean asymptotics, joint spatial CLT and spatial mean asymptotics, all-vertex localization, the critical-pole fixed-point CLT and mean asymptotics, uniformly bounded means for each longer critical cycle length, and the extension of all these conclusions to the interior grid. The Sukhatme specialization uses this extension and exact scaling of the finite law. The intermediate population, quantile, insertion, domination, trace, collision, factorial-moment, and martingale results are proof obligations, not assumptions.
